## Technische Universität Eisenfeld

<img src="https://api.tu-eisenfeld.de/storage/v1/object/public/site-assets/logo-1787781688632-transparente.png" alt="TU Eisenfeld" width="120" align="right">


Digital technical university in Eisenfeld (Saxony-Anhalt, Germany), founded in 2011 as a virtual teaching and research project.
Nine schools, 22 degree programmes on campus, online and hybrid.

- Website: https://tu-eisenfeld.de · Library & Working Paper Series: https://tu-eisenfeld.de/en/library
- Open metadata of the Working Paper Series: [`working-papers`](https://github.com/tu-eisenfeld/working-papers)
- Future Technologies Laboratory (FTL): https://ftl-tueisenfeld.de
- Press office: https://tu-eisenfeld.de/de/ueber-uns/presse · Campus community: https://tu-eisenfeld.de/campus · Hochschulwiki: https://hochschul-wiki.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Eisenfeld

Research focus: robotics and autonomous systems, computer and data science, engineering and energy technology, responsible design of digital technologies.
